WORKFLOW 1: Patient Registration & AI Pre-Screening
Contributor: Ahmad Murtaza
-------------------------------------------
Purpose: Demonstrates user onboarding + AI integration
Step-by-step:

1. Patient navigates to registration page
2. Selects "Patient" role and fills registration form (name, email, password, DOB, gender)
3. System validates input and creates account
4. Patient logs in with credentials
5. System prompts to complete medical profile (blood type, allergies, medical history)
6. Patient initiates AI pre-screening for appointment
7. AI chatbot (Gemini) asks screening questions based on symptoms
8. Patient provides responses to symptom questions
9. AI generates preliminary assessment and urgency level
10. System suggests appropriate doctor specialization and appointment priority
11. Patient receives confirmation and can proceed to book appointment

Key Features: Registration, Authentication, AI NLP Integration, Patient Profile Management

-------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW 2: Doctor's Daily Schedule & Patient Review
Contributor: Ahmad Murtaza
-------------------------------------------
Purpose: Demonstrates role-based dashboards + data access control
Step-by-step:

1. Doctor logs into the system
2. System displays role-specific dashboard with today's schedule
3. Doctor views list of scheduled appointments with patient names and time slots
4. Doctor selects a patient from the schedule
5. System displays patient's medical history, allergies, and AI pre-screening results
6. Doctor reviews AI-generated preliminary assessment
7. Doctor can view any uploaded medical images (X-rays) with AI flagged anomalies
8. Doctor adds consultation notes to patient record
9. Doctor updates appointment status (completed/rescheduled/cancelled)
10. System logs all actions in audit trail
11. Real-time notification sent to patient about appointment status update

Key Features: RBAC, Doctor Dashboard, Audit Logging, Real-time Notifications, Medical Records Access
